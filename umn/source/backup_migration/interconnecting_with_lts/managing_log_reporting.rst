Managing Log Reporting
======================

Scenarios
---------

After access logs are configured, all new log records generated by the DRS instance are uploaded to Log Tank Service (LTS) for management.

Precautions
-----------

-  After log reporting is complete, all logs of the task are uploaded to LTS by default.

-  The configuration does not take effect immediately. There is a delay of about 10 minutes.
-  Ensure that a log group and a log stream already exist in LTS in the same region as the DRS task.
-  No fees are charged after LTS log reporting is disabled.

Enabling or Disabling Log Reporting
-----------------------------------

#. Log in to the management console.
#. Click |image1| in the upper left corner and select a region and project.
#. Choose **Databases** > **Data Replication Service**. The **Data Replication Service** page is displayed.
#. In the navigation pane, choose **Backup Migration Management**. On the **Backup Migration Management** page, click the name of the target instance to go to the basic information page.
#. On the **Basic Information** page, click **Migration Logs** on the left.
#. Click |image2| next to **Report Logs to LTS** in the upper part of the page.
#. Select an LTS log group and log stream and click **OK**.

   .. note::

      The configuration does not take effect immediately. There is a delay of about 10 minutes.

8. To disable or modify log reporting, click the **Report Logs to LTS** toggle switch or click **Edit** next to it.

   -  Modifying log reporting: Click **Edit** next to the **Report Logs to LTS** toggle switch. In the displayed dialog box, select the LTS log group and log stream again and click **OK**.
   -  Disabling log reporting: Click the **Report Logs to LTS** toggle switch. In the displayed dialog box, click **OK**.
